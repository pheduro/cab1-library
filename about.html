About our library

Who we are, what we offer, and the standards that guide us.

Who we are

The heart of reading at Cabancalan I ES

Our school library is a separate room within a building. It is easily accessible from any point on the campus, safe from flooding, and can hold 50 to 75 learners at a time. It serves the school's 1,935 learners and its teachers.

The library is guided by DepEd Order No. 56, s. 2011 (Standards for Philippine Libraries) and DECS Order No. 006, s. 1998 (Policies and Programs for School Library Development).
