What we offer

Everything a young reader needs

From borrowing books to learning how to research, our library supports learners and teachers every day.

📕

Book borrowing

Learners may borrow books for home use and keep the reading going after school.

💻

Computers & internet

Computers for research, encoding and browsing, plus internet access for learners and faculty.

🪑

Reading corner

A cozy corner for independent reading or reading with friends, and space to study inside the library.

📱

E-blocks device

Learners can use the e-blocks device for digital learning and reading.

🌐

Three languages

Story books in Sinugboanong Binisaya, Filipino and English, plus young-reader novels.

📚

Reference collection

Encyclopedias, dictionaries and reference books in all subject areas for teachers and learners.

Our mission

Building a reading culture in every grade

Our library plan aims for a sustained, inclusive and learner-centered reading culture. We bring together independent reading, read-alouds, buddy reading, extra help for emerging readers, and school-wide reading events.

  • Easy to reach from anywhere on campus and safe from flooding
  • Open Monday to Friday, 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM, no noon break
  • Reading programs for Kindergarten to Grade 6
